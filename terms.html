Waypoint Terms of Use

Updated 29 October 2026

The short version

Waypoint is your private travel atlas. Your trips are yours. If you use the account features — friends, the Postcards feed, sharing, sync — be decent, post only what’s yours to post, and know that we can remove content and accounts that break the rules below. Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Everything else is detail.

Your account

You don’t need an account to use Waypoint. If you create one, you sign in with Apple and pick a @username and display name. Pick a name that is yours to use — no impersonating another person or a business you don’t represent. You are responsible for what happens under your account. One person, one account; if we suspend an account for breaking the rules, creating another one to get around it breaks them too.

Your content stays yours

Your trips, photos, walks, captions and comments belong to you. Waypoint claims no ownership of them, ever. To run the features you choose, you give Waypoint a limited licence to store, copy, transmit and display your content only as needed to do what you asked: show a postcard to your friends (or publicly, if you marked it public), deliver a shared trip to the friend you sent it to and keep the copy they accepted, and keep your sync copy in step across your devices. That licence ends when the content is deleted — by you, or when you delete your account — apart from copies a friend already accepted into their own atlas, which are theirs.

Content rules

The feed is friends-only, and small. Keep it that way:

— Don’t post spam, scams or advertising.
— Don’t harass, threaten or bully anyone, and don’t post hateful content aimed at people for who they are.
— Don’t post illegal content, or content that encourages illegal or dangerous behaviour.
— Don’t post other people’s private information — including someone else’s precise location or travel plans — without their clear OK.
— Don’t post photos or writing that aren’t yours to share.
— Don’t impersonate anyone, and don’t create accounts to evade a block or a ban.

Reports, blocks and moderation

Every postcard, comment and profile can be reported in the app, and you can block anyone — blocking cuts contact in both directions, everywhere in Waypoint. We review reports: safety issues (harassment, threats) the same day, spam and abuse within a day. We may remove content that breaks the rules above, hide reported comments while they are reviewed, and — for serious or repeated breaches — suspend or ban an account. Clear-cut violations are handled under a standing playbook; bans and edge cases are decided by a person, not an algorithm. If your content or account is actioned and you think we got it wrong, contact support and a human will take another look within two days.

Subscriptions and trials

Max is an auto-renewing subscription billed by Apple through your Apple ID. The launch offer is 1 month free on monthly billing and 3 months free on annual billing; the paywall states your first-charge date and amount before you start, and the app schedules a reminder before a trial converts. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before a period ends, it renews at the stated price. Manage or cancel any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions — deleting Waypoint or your account does not cancel billing. Refunds are Apple’s to give, under Apple’s policies.

Your atlas, export and deletion

You can export your whole atlas, free, on every plan, and we will never paywall your existing data. Deleting your account permanently removes what our servers hold for you, as described in the Privacy Policy; the atlas on your phone is yours and stays until you delete it. If Waypoint ever shuts down, we will say so in the app with reasonable notice — your local atlas and exports keep working regardless, because they never depended on our servers.

Availability and changes

We work to keep the account features up, but they are provided as-is, without a promise of uninterrupted service, and sync is a copy — your phone’s atlas is the original. If these terms change materially, the new version will appear in the app and at this URL with a new date, and continued use of the account features after that counts as accepting them.

Waypoint is a record of where you went, not a navigation or safety tool; don’t rely on it to get anywhere or to stay safe.
